Why Walrus?
Traditional audit reports live in PDFs or Google Docs — centralized, mutable, easily lost. SuiShield uses Walrus to store reports as permanent blobs, with a blob ID registered on Sui. Any user, integrator, or auditor can retrieve and verify the exact report that was generated at audit time. This creates an open, public audit history for the entire Sui DeFi ecosystem.
